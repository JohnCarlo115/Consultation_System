Help & Support Center

Find answers to common questions regarding consultation bookings and live queue tracking.

How do I book a consultation with my professor?

+

Navigate to Book Consultation from the sidebar. Choose your professor, pick an available date and time slot, type your consultation topic, and click Book Consultation.

How does the live consultation queue work?

+

Once your appointment is confirmed for the current day, your turn will appear under Consultation Queue. You can view your real-time queue position and estimated waiting time.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

+

Yes, go to My Appointments to view your active bookings and cancel or rebook if your selected schedule is no longer available.
